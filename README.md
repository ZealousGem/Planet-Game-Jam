# Planet-Game-Jam
Repo for the Abnormal Shift

Late Abnormal Shift, Survival Shooting Game

Synopsis

You play as an operator who oversees an exo-planet, his job is to find any abnormal creatures about to invade the settlements and shoot them down.  The player will keep shooting the enemies until there are too many.
Game Loop

The operator must shoot down the enemies before they reach the settlement. If the Settlement's health reaches zero then game over. This game will run based on a wave based system, and could add different settlements. 
Game Mechanics 

The player can move the camera around, trying to detect any creatures

There will be a variety of creatures, maybe even a boss for each final wave. 

Upgrade system player can use after beating a certain amount of enemies upgrades could come after each wave. 

Could have many settlements the player has to defend and not one.

Could be a mechanic where storms come in making it hazy for the player to be unable to  see a certain section. 

Could have a day/night cycle where enemies are stronger at night than in the day. 



Visual Look

Will be 2D top view 

Could contain different planets for players to oversee based on the level
